# VTT Structured Roll and Combat Rules

This is an independent dnd_sim product specification. It turns facts already
generated by this repository's rules engine into tabletop presentation. It does
not reproduce another VTT's source, schemas, identifiers, dice algorithms,
assets, or visual expression.

## Outcome

Players can understand authoritative attacks, saves, checks, healing, damage,
and mitigation without the browser rolling again. The GM can run the existing
engine encounter from accessible controls. Preview and commit show the same
facts for the same starting state, exact command replay never duplicates a
card, and private or blind facts are absent from unauthorized responses.

## Authority and lifecycle

- Only the rules engine generates roll facts. The VTT does not accept a client
  command that claims die faces, totals, hit state, save state, or applied
  damage.
- A roll record is finalized after every rule timing hook that can replace a
  face or total and, for damage, after target mitigation. An incomplete RNG
  boundary may remain in engine-local diagnostics but is never projected as a
  final card.
- Generated faces retain generation order and replacement links. Card IDs are
  deterministic engine journal IDs, not browser UUIDs.
- Preview executes against the session's cloned state and cloned RNG. Its roll
  card drafts must be byte-equivalent to the later commit from the same
  revision. Preview does not persist; commit records are part of the durable
  session event log and reconnect by event sequence.
- A rejected declaration, failed durable append, or incompatible restart
  restores state, RNG, and journal atomically.

## Final facts

The existing `engine.roll_journal.v1` remains the rules-kernel source. A final
record may contain:

- d20: every candidate/replacement face, the base flat modifier, signed
  post-roll total and threshold adjustments, every modifier die, the effective
  total/threshold, outcome, and critical state;
- saving throw or check: ability, DC when applicable, candidates, modifier,
  total, and success/failure;
- damage: expression, type, generated/replaced faces, rolled and raw totals,
  applied total, critical expansion, and signed adjustments for floors,
  timing reductions, resistance, vulnerability, immunity, absorption, and
  other engine rules. Captured packets correlate by stable source occurrence,
  never by a mutable post-hook amount;
- healing: expression or fixed source, generated faces, raw healing, effective
  healing, and overheal, once the engine owns an equivalent finalized fact.

Facts unsupported by the current engine are omitted rather than inferred from
HP changes, telemetry strings, or aggregate damage counters.

## Audience projection

Engine audience intent is actor-based: public, GM-only, or a canonical set of
actor IDs. At the VTT boundary it maps through the authenticated table roster:

- public is visible to every table participant, subject to hidden-actor
  projection;
- actor-scoped is visible to the GM and participants owning at least one listed
  actor;
- GM-only is visible only to the GM and implements a blind result;
- an unauthorized record is absent from HTTP, command receipts, SSE history,
  reconnect hydration, and browser state—not present with CSS concealment;
- source or target identities that are not visible to an authorized viewer are
  omitted from the card. The projection never sends the engine audience intent
  or hidden modifier provenance.

## Browser cards

`vtt.roll_card.v1` is renderer-neutral and strict. It carries the deterministic
card ID, event sequence/revision when committed, safe source/target IDs when
visible, action/purpose, roll kind, expression, generated faces and statuses,
total, optional threshold/outcome, critical state, and finalized damage stages.
Cards are ordered by durable event sequence and bounded per response. Unknown,
partial, unfinalized, extra-field, and incompatible cards fail closed. The
browser independently checks replacement chains, kept-face/mode rules, d20 and
save comparisons, and rolled-to-raw-to-applied arithmetic before rendering.

The browser renders semantic articles with a heading, plain-language outcome,
dice expression, total, expandable face provenance, damage adjustments, and a
live announcement for newly committed cards. Color is supplemental. Keyboard
users can expand details, and reduced-motion disables card entrance effects.

## Combat controls

- Existing engine commands remain the only initiative and turn authority:
  start encounter, submit the active actor declaration, and follow the engine's
  next prepared prompt.
- HP, initiative, conditions, target legality, action economy, and movement are
  projections, not editable browser truth.
- Add/remove combatants, initiative override, previous turn, HP override, and
  freeform condition override remain unavailable until each has an explicit,
  replayable engine command and snapshot invariant. UI controls must not imply
  support before then.

## Bounds and stable failures

- At most 256 roll records per engine transition, 64 generated faces per record,
  64 adjustments per record, and 500 hydrated cards per request.
- Engine and VTT codecs reject non-finite, unsorted, duplicate, partial,
  unfinalized, or over-budget data before persistence or projection.
- Protected authentication runs before cursor or query validation. Public
  failures are stable and do not echo hidden actors, actions, faces, or audience
  membership.

## Acceptance fixtures

- Fixed-seed normal/advantage/disadvantage, critical, reroll/replacement, save,
  check, resistance, immunity, vulnerability, and healing fixtures preserve RNG
  draw count and engine outcome with journaling enabled.
- Timing-hook replacements and modifier dice are reflected in the final fact;
  worse or tied alternatives remain visible, flat modifiers are not rewritten,
  and Shield records the effective AC threshold instead of an inferred miss.
- Attack and save damage reduced by a timing hook still produce one finalized
  fact per generated packet, with raw-stage adjustments whose applied sum
  reconciles to the authoritative HP change.
- Preview and commit card drafts are byte-identical; commit/restart/reconnect and
  exact retry yield one durable card per deterministic ID.
- Public, actor-private, self, and GM-only cards are tested against GM, owner,
  other player, and spectator. Hidden IDs/modifiers are absent from every
  unauthorized transport and browser object.
- Accessible browser tests cover semantic cards, detail toggles, live updates,
  reconnect, error/loading states, and reduced motion.
